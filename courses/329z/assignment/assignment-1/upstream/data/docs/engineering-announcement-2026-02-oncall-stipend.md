# Engineering Announcement: On-Call Stipend Increase

Posted: 2026-02-09
From: Tomas Lindqvist, CTO, and Grace Nwosu, Director of Site Reliability Engineering
To: Engineering

We are raising on-call pay. The new amounts apply to rotations that start on or after 2026-03-02.

| Role in the rotation | New stipend per week |
|---|---|
| Primary | $500 |
| Secondary | $200 |

An engineer who is primary or secondary during a week that contains a company holiday, or during the winter closure, receives an additional $250 for that week. The holiday week bonus is new.

These amounts replace Section 4 of the On-Call and Incident Process (effective 2025-06-01), which set the stipend at $350 for primary and $150 for secondary. Every other part of that document stays in force, including the severity levels, the response times, and the rule that no engineer is on call more than one week in four.

## Background

The stipend had not changed since the rotations were set up. In 2025 the number of connected meters grew from 0.9 million to 1.4 million, and the number of pages per primary week rose from an average of 3.1 to an average of 5.4. The SRE team has an alert-reduction project under way for the first half of 2026, and Hiro Matsuda will report on page volume at Incident Review each month. Pay should reflect the current load while that work is done.

## Practical details

- Stipends are paid through payroll in the pay period after the rotation week ends. You do not need to submit anything. Payroll takes the schedule from PagerLine.
- If you swap a shift, the person who carries the pager for the week receives the stipend. Record swaps in PagerLine before the handoff on Monday.
- The Forecasting rotation, which covers business hours only, is paid at the secondary rate as before.
- Compensatory time for night pages is unchanged.

Questions go to Hiro Matsuda or to your manager.
