# Announcement: Three In-Office Days for Hybrid Teams

Posted: 2026-05-04
From: Marcus Delgado, VP People
To: All employees

Starting 2026-06-01, hybrid employees will work from their assigned office at least 3 days each week. The anchor days will be Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday.

This replaces Section 3 of the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy (HR-110, effective 2025-04-01), which set the expectation at two days with Tuesday and Thursday as anchor days. It also replaces the summary in Section 7 of the Employee Handbook. The rest of the policy stays in force.

## Why

The leadership team reviewed a year of data with People Operations and Workplace. On the two anchor days the Oakland office was close to full, and on the other days it was nearly empty. Teams reported that two days were not enough to fit planning, design reviews, and one-on-ones, and new hires said they rarely met people outside their own team. Adding Wednesday gives a continuous block in the middle of the week.

## Details

- The change applies to hybrid employees in Oakland, Boulder, and Rotterdam.
- Remote and on-site arrangements do not change.
- Monday and Friday remain flexible. If you come in on those days, book a desk in DeskSpot as before.
- PTO, sick leave, business travel, and customer visits on an anchor day still count toward the expectation.
- Standing exceptions that People Operations has already approved remain valid until their next six-month review.
- The home office stipend, the internet stipend for remote employees, and the 20 days of work-from-anywhere time are unchanged.

## Office changes

Rosa Alvarez and the Workplace team are adding assigned desks on the third floor of the Oakland office during May so that there is room for everyone on all three anchor days. Part of the third floor stays as shared seating that you book in DeskSpot. If your desk changes, you will receive your new assignment by May 22.

Talk to your manager first. For exceptions, contact People Operations at people@cardinal-energy.example. We will hold two question-and-answer sessions on Perch Live, on May 7 and May 12.
