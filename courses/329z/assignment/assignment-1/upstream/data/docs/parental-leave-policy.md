# Parental Leave Policy

Policy number: HR-104
Effective: 2025-01-01
Owner: People Operations
Approved by: Marcus Delgado, VP People

## 1. Purpose

Cardinal Energy provides paid leave so that employees can care for and bond with a new child. This policy covers employees in the United States. Rotterdam employees are covered by Dutch statutory leave and the Netherlands addendum.

## 2. Eligibility

You are eligible for paid parental leave if you meet all of these conditions:

- You are a full-time or part-time employee. Contractors and interns are not covered.
- You have completed six months of employment on the date of the birth or placement.
- You become a parent through birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child under 18.

## 3. Length of leave

- Birthing parents receive 16 weeks of paid leave.
- Non-birthing parents receive 12 weeks of paid leave.

If both parents work at Cardinal Energy, each receives their own leave. The length of leave is per birth or placement event. It does not increase for twins or for the placement of more than one child at the same time.

## 4. Pay and benefits during leave

Leave is paid at 100 percent of base salary. Where a state program or disability insurance pays part of your wages during leave, the company pays the difference so that you receive your full base salary. Employees on a variable compensation plan are paid according to the leave provisions of that plan.

Benefits continue during leave as if you were working. Stock options continue to vest. PTO continues to accrue during paid parental leave.

## 5. Timing

Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement. You may take it as one continuous period or split it into at most 2 blocks. Each block must be at least two weeks long. A birthing parent may start leave up to four weeks before the expected due date.

## 6. Notice

Tell People Operations at least 60 days before the expected date of birth or placement, or as soon as you can if that much notice is not possible. People Operations will meet with you to explain pay, state programs, and the forms to complete. Tell your manager at the same time so that the team can plan coverage.

## 7. Phased return

When you return from a block of leave of eight weeks or more, you may work a reduced schedule of 80 percent for the first 4 weeks at full pay. Agree on the schedule with your manager before you return.

## 8. Job protection

You return to the same role, or to an equivalent role with the same pay and level. Taking parental leave has no effect on performance ratings, and a review period that includes leave is assessed on the time worked.

## 9. Pregnancy loss

An employee who experiences a pregnancy loss, and the employee's partner, may take up to 10 days of paid leave. Contact People Operations. No documentation is required.

## 10. Related leave

Unpaid family and medical leave under federal and state law runs at the same time as paid parental leave where the law allows. You may add PTO to the end of parental leave with your manager's approval.
