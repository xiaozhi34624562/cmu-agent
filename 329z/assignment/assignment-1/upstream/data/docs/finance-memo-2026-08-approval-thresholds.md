# Finance Memo: Expense Approval Thresholds

Posted: 2026-08-17
Effective: 2026-09-01
From: Helen Okafor, CFO
To: All employees
Cc: Peter Andersson, Controller

Travel spending in the first half of 2026 ran 14 percent over plan. Most of the overrun came from individual expenses between $750 and $1,000, which under the March policy reach Finance with only a manager's approval. After discussion with the leadership team, I am making two changes to approvals.

## Change 1: vice president approval

From 2026-09-01, any single expense over $750 requires approval from your department's vice president, in addition to your manager.

This replaces the $1,000 threshold in Section 14 of the Travel and Expense Policy, version 2026.1 (effective 2026-03-01).

## Change 2: pre-trip approval

From 2026-09-01, any trip with an estimated total cost over $2,500 needs your manager's approval in TripDesk before you book. TripDesk will show the estimate and request the approval automatically. This rule is new. Trips to customer sites for installations or incident response are exempt.

## What does not change

- The CFO approval threshold stays at $5,000.
- All spending limits in the March policy stay as they are, including lodging, meals, mileage, and customer entertainment.
- The 45-day submission deadline and the receipt rules stay as they are.

Ledgerly has been updated and will route reports under the new threshold for expenses dated September 1 or later. Expenses dated before September 1 follow the old threshold even if you submit them later.

The Controller will reissue the policy document with these changes at the next annual revision. Until then, this memo governs approvals.
