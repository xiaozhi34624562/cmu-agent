# Benefits Summary, Plan Year 2026

Effective: 2026-01-01
Owner: People Operations
Contact: Imani Brooks, Benefits Specialist (benefits@cardinal-energy.example)

This summary lists the benefits for US employees of Cardinal Energy for the 2026 plan year. The plan documents govern if they differ from this summary. Rotterdam employees have a separate summary under the Netherlands addendum.

## Enrollment

New employees have 30 days from their start date to enroll. Coverage starts on the first day of employment. Open enrollment for the 2026 plan year ran from November 3 to November 21, 2025. Outside open enrollment you can change your elections only within 30 days of a qualifying life event.

## Health insurance

We offer three medical plans: a PPO plan, an HMO plan, and a high-deductible plan with a health savings account. Cardinal Energy pays 90 percent of the premium for the employee and 75 percent of the premium for dependents on all three plans. Employees who choose the high-deductible plan receive a company contribution of $1,000 per year to the health savings account, paid in equal parts each pay period.

Dental and vision plans are offered with the same company share of the premium.

## Retirement

All employees may contribute to the 401(k) plan from their first paycheck. Cardinal Energy matches 100 percent of your contributions up to the first 4 percent of your salary. The match vests immediately. You can choose traditional or Roth contributions. New employees are enrolled automatically at 4 percent and may change the rate at any time in the plan provider's portal.

## Insurance

- Life insurance: two times your annual base salary, paid by the company.
- Short-term and long-term disability insurance, paid by the company.
- Optional additional life insurance at your own cost.

## Wellness allowance

Cardinal Energy reimburses up to $65 per month for expenses that support your physical and mental health. Eligible expenses include fitness facility dues, exercise classes, sports league fees, meditation applications, and home exercise equipment. Submit the expense in Ledgerly under the Wellness category. The allowance is taxable. Unused amounts do not roll over from month to month.

## Professional development allowance

Each full-time employee may spend up to $1,800 per calendar year on learning that relates to their current role or a role they are working toward. Eligible expenses include event registration fees, coursework, certification examinations, and books. Your manager approves each use in Ledgerly. Travel to an event is not charged to this allowance. It is business travel under the Travel and Expense Policy. The allowance does not carry over, and it is prorated in your first year by quarter of hire.

## Commuter benefit

Hybrid and on-site employees receive $100 per month toward public transit or vanpool costs, loaded onto a commuter card. You may add your own pre-tax contributions up to the legal limit. Parking at the Oakland office is covered in the Oakland Office Guide.

## Phone stipend

Employees in roles that carry a pager or that work mainly with customers receive a phone stipend of $40 per month. Your manager can tell you whether your role qualifies.

## Employee assistance program

The employee assistance program gives you and the members of your household 8 free counseling sessions per person per year, along with legal and financial advice lines. The service is confidential, and the company receives no information about who uses it.

## Family benefits

- Paid parental leave: see the Parental Leave Policy and any later announcements.
- Dependent care flexible spending account, funded with your pre-tax contributions.
- Backup childcare: 10 days per year through our provider, at a copay of $20 per day.

## Time off

Paid time off, holidays, sick leave, and other leave are described in the PTO Policy and the Employee Handbook.
