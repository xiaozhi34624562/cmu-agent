# New Hire Onboarding Guide

Updated: 2026-06-15
Owner: People Operations (Tessa Nguyen, Director of People Operations)
Audience: New employees and their managers

Welcome to Cardinal Energy. This guide covers your first 90 days: what happens when, what you need to do, and who can help. Your manager has a matching checklist in PeopleHub.

## Before your first day

- You will receive an email from People Operations with a link to PeopleHub. Complete your tax forms, your direct deposit details, and your emergency contact there.
- IT ships a laptop to remote employees so that it arrives in the week before the start date. Hybrid and on-site employees collect their laptop on the first morning.
- Your manager will send you your first-week schedule and the name of your onboarding buddy.
- Bring identification for employment verification. The verification must be completed within 3 business days of your start date.

## Your first day

New employees start on a Monday. If you are hybrid or on-site, arrive at your office at 09:30. In Oakland, ask for the Workplace team at the reception desk on the ground floor of 1450 Harborline Street. They will take your badge photo and show you to your desk. Remote employees join the welcome call at 09:30 Pacific.

IT setup takes about an hour. Felix Moreau or another member of the IT team will help you sign in, set up multi-factor authentication, and install VaultKey. Choose a password of at least 14 characters.

Your manager takes you to lunch, or sends you a meal voucher if you are remote.

## Cardinal 101

Orientation is called Cardinal 101. It runs on the Monday afternoon and the Tuesday of your first week. The sessions are:

- The company and its customers, with a member of the executive team.
- How the grid works, for people new to the energy industry. Lila Haddad's team teaches this session, and most new hires say it is the most useful one.
- Product tour: GridLens, the Meridian M4, and the Pulse S2.
- Benefits and time off, with Imani Brooks.
- Security basics, with the Security team.

Remote employees are encouraged to travel to Oakland for the first week. The trip is business travel. The meal limit for domestic travel is $75 per day, and you book through TripDesk once your account is active. People Operations books the first trip for you if your start date is too close.

## Your first week

- Meet your buddy. Your buddy is a colleague outside your direct team who answers the questions you would not think to ask your manager. The buddy arrangement lasts 6 weeks.
- Agree on your 30-60-90 day plan with your manager.
- Read the Employee Handbook. Note that announcements posted after the handbook's effective date take precedence over it. People Operations keeps a list of current announcements on Perch.
- Set up your profile in PeopleHub and on Perch.
- Join the channels for your team and your office.

## Your first 30 days

- Enroll in benefits. You have 30 days from your start date. After that you must wait for open enrollment.
- Complete security awareness training. It is due within 30 days of joining.
- Complete harassment prevention training. It is due within 60 days, and most people do it in the first month.
- Your home office stipend of $500 arrives with your first full paycheck if you are hybrid or remote.
- Your first payday depends on your start date. The Compensation and payroll section of the Employee Handbook explains the schedule.
- Have a first check-in with your manager about the 30-day goals in your plan.

## Where you work

Your offer letter states whether your role is hybrid, remote, or on-site. Hybrid employees work from their assigned office at least 3 days each week, and the anchor days are Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. Your desk assignment comes from the Workplace team. If you want to come in on a Monday or a Friday, book a desk in DeskSpot.

## Time off in your first year

You accrue PTO from your first day at a rate of 18 days per year. You may take PTO during your introductory period with your manager's approval. You also have 10 days of sick leave and 2 floating holidays per calendar year. At the end of the year you may carry over up to 10 days of unused PTO.

## For engineers

Engineering has its own checklist in addition to this guide.

- Day 1 to 3: set up your development environment using the setup script in the engineering handbook repository. If the script fails, tell the Platform team, because a failure is treated as a defect in the script.
- Week 1: merge your first change. Your manager will have chosen a small starter task.
- Week 2: read the GridLens Architecture Overview and attend an Incident Review as an observer.
- Firmware and hardware engineers complete electrical safety training before entering the high-voltage test bay in Boulder.
- Engineers with production access receive two hardware security keys from IT.
- After 60 days: shadow one full week of on-call with an experienced primary.
- After 90 days: join your team's on-call rotation.

## For people in customer-facing roles

- Week 1: read the GridLens Product FAQ. It has the approved answers to common customer questions.
- Week 2 and 3: sit in on four customer calls with a colleague.
- Week 4: give the standard product demonstration to your manager.
- Sales hires complete pricing and deal desk training with Jamal Whitfield's team before sending a first quote.

## The end of the introductory period

The first 90 days are your introductory period. At the end, your manager writes a short check-in that reviews your 30-60-90 day plan. You will also be asked to fill in a survey about onboarding. We read every response, and this guide changes because of them.
