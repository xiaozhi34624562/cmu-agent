# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Policy number: HR-110
Effective: 2025-04-01
Owner: People Operations, with Workplace
Approved by: Marcus Delgado, VP People

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out where Cardinal Energy employees work. It defines the three work arrangements, the expectations for each, and the support the company provides for working from home.

## 2. Work arrangements

Every role has one of three arrangements. Your offer letter states yours, and PeopleHub shows it on your profile.

**Hybrid.** You live within 50 miles of a Cardinal office (Oakland, Boulder, or Rotterdam) and split your time between that office and home. Most employees are hybrid.

**Remote.** You work from a home office and do not have an assigned desk. A role is remote only if it was posted as remote or if your department's vice president and People Operations have approved the arrangement in writing.

**On-site.** The work can only be done at a company site. Lab technicians, hardware test engineers during build weeks, and Workplace staff are on-site.

## 3. Expectations for hybrid employees

Hybrid employees work from their assigned office at least 2 days each week. The anchor days are Tuesday and Thursday. On anchor days, teams schedule planning meetings, design reviews, and one-on-ones so that time in the office is spent with colleagues.

You may come in on more days if you wish. On days other than anchor days, book a desk in DeskSpot, because some neighborhoods are set up as shared seating.

If you cannot come in on an anchor day, tell your manager ahead of time. An occasional absence needs no formal approval. A standing exception, for example for a medical reason or a caregiving duty, is requested through People Operations and reviewed every six months.

Time spent on PTO, sick leave, business travel, or at a customer site on an anchor day counts as meeting the expectation.

## 4. Expectations for remote employees

Remote employees work during the core hours of the office their team is attached to. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in that office's local time.

Remote employees travel to an office for a team week once per quarter, four times a year. The team's manager chooses the dates at least six weeks ahead. Travel for team weeks is business travel under the Travel and Expense Policy.

A remote employee who moves to within 50 miles of an office becomes hybrid on the first day of the following quarter.

## 5. Expectations for on-site employees

On-site employees work at their site on every scheduled working day. A manager may allow an on-site employee to work from home on a day when the work allows it, for example for training or documentation.

## 6. Home office support

- New hybrid and remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500, paid with the first full paycheck. It is taxable income. Use it for a desk, chair, monitor, or similar items. You do not need to submit receipts.
- Remote employees receive an internet stipend of $75 per month.
- IT provides a laptop, a headset, and one external monitor on request. Equipment remains company property.
- Hybrid employees do not receive the internet stipend.

An ergonomic assessment of your home office is available through Workplace. Request it at workplace@cardinal-energy.example.

## 7. Working from another location

You may work from a location other than your home or assigned office for up to 20 working days per calendar year. This is called work-from-anywhere time. The rules are:

- The location must be in your country of employment. Working from another country needs approval from Legal at least 30 days ahead, because it can create tax and immigration obligations for you and for the company.
- You must be available during your team's core hours.
- You must have a private, secure internet connection. Public Wi-Fi without the VPN is not acceptable.
- Employees who hold production access or who handle Restricted data must tell Security before working from a new location.

Tell your manager the dates before you go, and record them in PeopleHub under Work Location.

## 8. Changing your arrangement

To change from hybrid to remote, or the reverse, talk to your manager. The change needs approval from your department's vice president and People Operations. Pay ranges are set by location. If you move to a different pay region, your salary may be adjusted at the next annual review.

## 9. Security at home

The IT and Security Policy applies wherever you work. Lock your screen when you step away. Do not let family members or guests use your company laptop. Do not print Confidential or Restricted material at home.

## 10. Review

People Operations and Workplace review this policy each year using office attendance data from the badge system and the results of the employee survey. Changes are announced on Perch.
