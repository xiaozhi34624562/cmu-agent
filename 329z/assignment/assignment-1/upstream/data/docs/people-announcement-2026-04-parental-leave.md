# Announcement: Expanded Parental Leave

Posted: 2026-04-20
From: Marcus Delgado, VP People
To: All US employees

I am glad to share an improvement to our parental leave benefit, approved by the leadership team last week.

## The new benefit

For births and placements on or after 2026-07-01:

- Every new parent receives 18 weeks of paid parental leave. The length no longer depends on whether you are the birthing parent.
- You are eligible after 90 days of employment.
- You may split the leave into up to 3 blocks, each at least two weeks long.

These terms replace Sections 2, 3, and 5 of the Parental Leave Policy (HR-104, effective 2025-01-01), which gave 16 weeks to birthing parents and 12 weeks to non-birthing parents, required six months of employment, and allowed two blocks. The summary in Section 15 of the Employee Handbook is also replaced.

## What stays the same

- Leave is paid at 100 percent of base salary.
- Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement.
- Please tell People Operations at least 60 days ahead.
- The phased return is unchanged: an 80 percent schedule for the first 4 weeks back, at full pay.
- Rotterdam colleagues remain covered by Dutch statutory leave and the Netherlands addendum.

## Births and placements before July 1

If your child is born or placed before 2026-07-01, the 2025 policy applies to you. If you are in this situation and have questions, Imani Brooks in People Operations can walk you through your options.

## Why we made this change

Two things drove the decision. First, the old policy gave non-birthing parents four fewer weeks, and employees told us that the difference did not match how their families share care. Second, several recent hires became parents before reaching six months of employment and had no paid leave at all.

Thank you to everyone who gave feedback.
