# Oakland Office Guide

Updated: 2026-02-02
Owner: Workplace (Rosa Alvarez, Workplace Manager)
Contact: workplace@cardinal-energy.example

## Address and hours

Cardinal Energy headquarters is at 1450 Harborline Street, Oakland, CA 94607. We occupy the ground floor reception and floors two through four.

The building is open to employees with a badge from 06:00 to 21:00 on weekdays. Outside those hours and at weekends, your badge opens the side entrance on Dockside Alley, and you must sign in on the tablet by the door so that we know who is in the building in an emergency. The reception desk is staffed from 08:30 to 17:30.

## Badges

Wear your badge while you are in the office. If you forget it, reception will issue a day badge. If you lose it, tell Workplace the same day so that it can be turned off. A replacement costs nothing the first time.

## Desks and rooms

- Floors two and four have assigned desks. Part of the third floor is shared seating that you book in DeskSpot.
- Meeting rooms are booked through the calendar. Rooms are released if nobody checks in on the room panel within 10 minutes of the start.
- There are six phone rooms on each floor. They cannot be booked.
- The quiet room on the fourth floor is for focused work. No calls and no conversations.
- The wellness room on the second floor is for nursing parents, prayer, and rest. Book it in DeskSpot.

## Visitors

Register visitors in the visitor system at least 24 hours before they arrive. Visitors sign a confidentiality acknowledgment at reception and wear a visitor badge. Stay with your visitor while they are in the building. Visitors use the guest Wi-Fi network, and the current password is on the screen at the reception desk.

## Getting here

- Public transit: the office is an 8-minute walk from the nearest regional rail station. The commuter benefit is described in the Benefits Summary.
- Bicycles: the bike room is on the ground floor behind reception and has 60 spaces, lockers, and two showers.
- Parking: the garage under the building has 40 spaces reserved for Cardinal Energy. The daily rate is $12, charged through the garage's application. A monthly permit costs $140 and is sold on the first working day of each month. The garage has 8 electric vehicle chargers, and a charging session is limited to 4 hours.

Parking costs for your normal commute are not reimbursable.

## Food and drink

Coffee, tea, fruit, and snacks are free in the kitchen on each floor. Lunch is catered on Thursdays and is served at 12:00 in the third-floor commons. Sign up by Tuesday at noon so that Workplace can order the right amount. There is no gym or cafeteria in the building, and the nearest places to eat are listed on the Workplace page of Perch.

## Mail and deliveries

Business mail and packages are received at reception and delivered to the mail shelves on each floor by 14:00. Outgoing shipments of company equipment go through Workplace. Bring the item to reception with a HelpDesk ticket number.

## Safety

- Emergency exits are marked on the floor plans next to each stairwell. The assembly point is the plaza on the north side of the building.
- Each floor has two volunteer floor wardens, a first aid kit, and a defibrillator by the kitchen.
- We hold an evacuation drill twice a year.
- In an earthquake: drop, cover, and hold on. Do not use the elevators afterward.
- Report hazards and injuries to Workplace on the same day.

## Dogs and other animals

Service animals are welcome. Other animals are not allowed in the office, because the building's lease does not permit them.

## Things that are not allowed

Space heaters, candles, and personal refrigerators. Bicycles and scooters on the office floors. Propping open a badge-controlled door.

## Requests

For a broken chair, a missing monitor cable, a room that is too hot, or a new desk setup, write to workplace@cardinal-energy.example or open a ticket in the Workplace queue of the HelpDesk portal. Most requests are handled within two working days.
